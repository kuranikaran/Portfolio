**Last updated:** 11 October 2026

## Report a security concern privately

Email **karankurani3k@gmail.com**, with subject line `Private vulnerability disclosure - [affected product]`.

Please do not publish unpatched exploit details in a public GitHub issue. Do not send live secrets, production user data, or offensive tooling as initial attachments. If sensitive evidence is necessary, request a secure transfer arrangement first.

Include the product and version, a concise vulnerability description, impact, reproducible steps using authorized test environments, any relevant patch or commit reference, a safe proof of concept, and the vendor's preferred disclosure contact if known.

## What this service does

I independently research security vulnerabilities and may, at my discretion, assist with evidence review, vendor notification, remediation validation and responsible publication. **I am not a CVE Numbering Authority (CNA).** CVE assignment requests must currently be made through an authorized CNA with appropriate scope.

This is a research disclosure contact, **not authorization to test third-party systems**. Vendors and software maintainers retain their own disclosure policies and legal rights. No bounty or payment is offered or promised.

## Expected response

I aim to acknowledge a well-formed private report within **5 business days**, and provide an initial triage or coordination update within **10 business days**. These are voluntary targets, not contractual guarantees.
